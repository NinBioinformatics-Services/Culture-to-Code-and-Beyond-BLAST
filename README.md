## 📌 Course Overview

**Culture to Code and Beyond 'BLAST'** is a university-aligned, 60 Hours MOOC designed to transition life science students from traditional wet-lab research into dry-lab computational workflows.

While basic database navigation and simple alignment tools get you started, modern microbiology and biotechnology demand expertise in high-throughput sequencing, 3D structural modeling, and systems-level interaction networks. This course bridges that critical gap.

## 📚 Syllabus

### Module 1: Multiple Sequence Alignment & Microbial Taxonomy
* **Key Topics:** MSA algorithms (CLUSTAL Omega, MUSCLE), 16S rRNA marker gene profiling, ITS sequencing for fungi, molecular phylogenetics (Neighbor-Joining, Maximum Likelihood), and bootstrapping analysis.
* **Practical Focus:** Constructing and interpreting evolutionary trees from raw gene sequences.

### Module 2: Structural Bioinformatics & Protein Modeling
* **Key Topics:** Protein structural levels, secondary structure prediction, 3D structure visualization, homology modeling (SWISS-MODEL), AI structure prediction (AlphaFold), and computational enzymology.
* **Practical Focus:** Active site mapping and catalytic residue analysis.

### Module 3: Microbial Genomics, NGS & Transcriptomics
* **Key Topics:** Illumina vs. Oxford Nanopore platforms, *de novo* assembly vs. reference mapping, open reading frame (ORF) annotation, RNA-Seq differential gene expression, and metagenomic sampling.
* **Practical Focus:** Processing high-throughput sequencing data pipelines.

### Module 4: Computer-Aided Drug Design & Systems Biology
* **Key Topics:** Molecular docking (AutoDock), *in silico* screening for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) genes, metabolic pathway reconstruction (KEGG, BioCyc), and host-pathogen interaction networks.
* **Practical Focus:** Simulating drug-target and antibiotic interactions.
